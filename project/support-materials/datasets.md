# Dataset Options

- [Steam Video Game and Bundle Data - Item metadata](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~jmcauley/datasets.html#steam_data) (20.2MB)
  - These datasets contain reviews from the Steam video game platform, and information about which games were bundled together.
  - [GitHub](https://github.com/bcc008/ucsd-dse-capstone-c4g4)
  - `publisher` `genres` `app_name` `title` `url` `tags` `discount_price` `reviews_url` `specs` `price` `early_access` `id` `developer`
- [Steam Reviews](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andrewmvd/steam-reviews) (2.16GB)
  - 6.4 million game reviews
  - `app_id` `app_name` `review_text` `review_score` `review_votes`
- [Steam Reviews Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/luthfim/steam-reviews-dataset) (120.72MB)
  - Collection of Steam's best selling games reviews
  - `date_posted` `funny` `helpful` `hour_played` `is_early_access_review` `recommendation` `review` `title`
- [Steam Reviews Dataset 2021](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/najzeko/steam-reviews-2021) (8.17GB)
  - Large collection of reviews of Steam games
  - `#` `app_id` `app_name` `review_id` `language` `review` `timestamp_created` `recommended` `votes_helpful`
